Frequency Tracker

MajoranaPropagation.MajoranaFrequencyTracker — Type
MajoranaFrequencyTracker(coeff)

A PathProperties wrapper around a coefficient of type CT that additionally tracks how many times the coefficient was multiplied by a cosine or sine during propagation: freq counts both, ncos the cosines, and nsins the sines. Used for the max_freq and max_sins truncations.

source
MajoranaPropagation.reset_tracker! — Method
reset_tracker!(msum::MajoranaSum{TT,MajoranaFrequencyTracker{CT}}) where {TT<:Integer,CT}

Reset the freq, nsins, and ncos counters of every coefficient of msum to zero in-place, keeping the coefficient values.

source
MajoranaPropagation.wrapcoefficients — Method
wrapcoefficients(msum::MajoranaSum, ::Type{MProp}) where {MProp<:PathProperties}

Return a new MajoranaSum with every coefficient wrapped into the PathProperties type MProp, e.g. MajoranaFrequencyTracker. The type MProp must provide a single-argument constructor MProp(coeff).
